Our Services

Secure handling. Clear shipment records.

We coordinate cross-border shipments with documented handling and online status updates. Customs inspections are conducted by the relevant authorities and can affect transit times.

MIPL / HANDLING

Mexico to Canada

The planned route includes two customs stops.

For shipments on this lane, the box transits through the United States for customs inspection and is reviewed again by Canadian customs before delivery.

01 / ORIGIN

Mexico

Shipment intake, identification, sealed handling, and preparation of applicable shipping documentation.

02 / TRANSIT

United States customs

The shipment stops for customs inspection while in transit through the United States.

03 / ENTRY

Canadian customs

A second customs review takes place in Canada before the shipment continues toward its destination.

04 / DELIVERY

Canada

Shipment status and chain-of-custody records continue through the delivery stage.

Customs inspections and clearance decisions are made by government authorities, not MIPL. Inspection requirements and transit times can vary by shipment.
