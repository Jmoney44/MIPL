Who We Are

A shipment should have a clear record at every handoff.

Mexican International Protocol Logistics (MIPL) is a private cross-border logistics service based in Silao de la Victoria, Guanajuato, Mexico. MIPL coordinates secure, documented shipments, including sealed-package handling, shipment identification, customs documentation, chain-of-custody records, and online tracking.

Handling

Sealed-package handling and shipment identification.

Documentation

Customs paperwork and shipment records.

Custody

Chain-of-custody records for key handoffs.

Visibility

Online tracking updates for shipment progress.
