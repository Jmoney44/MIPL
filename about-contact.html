Who We Are

Secure, documented shipments between Mexico and international destinations.

Mexican International Protocol Logistics (MIPL) is a private cross-border logistics service based in Silao de la Victoria, Guanajuato, Mexico. MIPL specializes in secure, documented shipments between Mexico, Canada, the United States, South America, and Europe. Services include sealed-package handling, shipment identification, customs documentation, chain-of-custody records, and online tracking.
